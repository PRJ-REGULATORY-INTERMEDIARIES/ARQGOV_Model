FUNDAMENTAL DISTINCTION

Relationship ≠ mechanism.

A delegation relationship, for example, may vary in direction, autonomy, intensity, accountability and sanctions. It should not be compressed into a generic R → I → T arrow.

METHODOLOGICAL CONTRIBUTION

Intermediation as a multidimensional property.

The number of intermediaries alone cannot describe an architecture: functions, legal authority, discretion and structural position also matter.
